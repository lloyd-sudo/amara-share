Sample member journey · fully tailored

Fairways of the Cape · 9 days

Days 1–3
Cape Town · Constantia

The Mother City & the Constantia fairways

Private arrival and a stay among the vineyards beneath Table Mountain, with the Winelands, the Peninsula and the Waterfront within easy reach.

⛳ Golf — Cape Royal Golf Estate & Westlake Golf Club: a modern layout and a classic parkland round beneath the mountain.
Days 4–6
Hermanus · The Whale Coast

The whale coast & Arabella

A scenic drive to a cliff-top hotel above the bay — in season, southern right whales drift within sight of the terrace. Sea-facing spa and coastal wine valley between rounds.

⛳ Golf — Arabella Golf Club: ranked among South Africa's very best, laid along the Bot River lagoon beneath the Kogelberg.
Days 7–9
Plettenberg Bay · Garden Route

The Garden Route finale

East along the Garden Route to a cliffside address above the bay — beaches, the Robberg reserve and the lagoon, with a relaxed final round before the flight home.

⛳ Golf — Plettenberg Bay Country Club: a friendly, walkable Garden-Route course among milkwoods and fynbos.
