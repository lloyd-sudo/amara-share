Days 01 – 02
Arrival · Table Mountain
Day 01

Arrival at the Cape

Private transfer · your Atlantic-seaboard home

You are met on arrival at Cape Town International and privately transferred to your home on the Atlantic seaboard — an interleading suite above the sea at The Clarendon, Fresnaye. The afternoon is yours to settle, unpack and rest after the flight.

As the sun sets over the ocean, dinner is arranged to your taste — in-suite with a private chef, or at one of the seaboard's finest tables, halal options mapped in advance.

OvernightThe Clarendon, FresnayeCape Town · Atlantic seaboard
Day 02

Table Mountain & the City

Cableway · Waterfront · Bo-Kaap

This morning, ascend Table Mountain by cableway — the whole Cape unfolding beneath you, from the city bowl to the two oceans. Later, explore the V&A Waterfront: harbour, boutiques and the aquarium the children will love.

End in the Bo-Kaap, the historic Cape-Malay quarter — cobbled lanes of colour, centuries-old mosques and some of the city's most authentic halal kitchens.

OvernightThe Clarendon, FresnayeCape Town · Atlantic seaboard

Days 03 – 04
The Peninsula · Constantia
Day 03

Cape Point & the Peninsula

Penguins · the Cape of Good Hope

A full day along one of the world's great coastal drives. Follow Chapman's Peak above the Atlantic to the Cape of Good Hope, where two oceans meet, then to the colony of African penguins at Boulders Beach — a family favourite.

Lunch is arranged at a harbour-side table, with the afternoon at a gentle pace back to the city.

OvernightThe Clarendon, FresnayeCape Town · Atlantic seaboard
Day 04

Into Constantia

Kirstenbosch · the Cape's oldest wine valley

A short transfer brings you to the green Constantia Valley. Wander the world-renowned Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens beneath the eastern slopes of Table Mountain, then settle into your private villa at The Cellars-Hohenort, set in nine acres of gardens.

The afternoon is unhurried — a swim, the spa, or simply the quiet of the oldest wine valley in the Cape.

OvernightThe Cellars-HohenortConstantia Valley

Days 05 – 06
Winelands · Leisure
Day 05

The Cape Winelands

Heritage estates · the duck parade

Today, the winelands. In Somerset West, the restored heritage estate of Vergenoegd-Low welcomes you with manicured gardens and its celebrated runner-duck parade — a delight for children and grandparents alike.

Estate lunches, grape-juice tastings for non-drinkers, and time among the vineyards make for a gentle, memorable day before returning to your Constantia villa.

OvernightThe Cellars-HohenortConstantia Valley
Day 06

Leisure at the Cape

Beaches · spa · optional whales

A free day to enjoy the Cape at your own rhythm — the white sands of Camps Bay and Clifton, a spa afternoon, or boutique shopping. In season (June–November), we can arrange a day on the whale coast at Hermanus, among the best land-based whale watching on earth.
