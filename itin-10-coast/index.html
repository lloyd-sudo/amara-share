Day 01

Arrival · Cape Town

Private transfer · your family villa

Welcomed at Cape Town International, you are privately transferred to your boutique villa above Bantry Bay — room for everyone, a pool, and the Atlantic laid out below. The rest of the day is for settling in after the flight.

OvernightThe Clarendon, Bantry BayCape Town
Day 02

Table Mountain & the Waterfront

Cableway · harbour · oceans

The cableway lifts you to the flat summit of Table Mountain and the Cape spread beneath. The afternoon belongs to the V&A Waterfront — the harbour, the two-oceans aquarium and easy, family-friendly dining with halal options close at hand.

OvernightThe Clarendon, Bantry BayCape Town

Days 03 – 04
The Peninsula · Winelands
Day 03

Cape Point & the penguins

Boulders Beach · the Cape of Good Hope

A day for the children to remember — the colony of African penguins at Boulders Beach, the wild headland of the Cape of Good Hope, and the coastal theatre of Chapman's Peak on the way home.

OvernightThe Clarendon, Bantry BayCape Town
Day 04

The Cape Winelands

Vergenoegd-Low · the duck parade

Out to Somerset West and the heritage estate of Vergenoegd-Low, where hundreds of runner ducks parade across the gardens each day — pure delight for the little ones. Estate lunch and grape-juice tastings round out a gentle day.

OvernightThe Clarendon, Bantry BayCape Town

Days 05 – 06
The Whale Coast · Garden Route
Day 05

Along the whale coast

Hermanus & Walker Bay · onward east

Leave the city and travel the coast eastward. In season (June–November) we pause at Hermanus, where southern right whales come close to shore — some of the finest land-based whale watching in the world — before continuing along the Garden Route.

OvernightEn route · Garden Routeprivate chauffeured journey

The Garden Route

Herolds Bay · a clifftop villa

Arrive at Waves of Grace — your exclusive-use villa on the cliffs above Herolds Bay, the Indian Ocean breaking below. Settle in, let the children find the beach, and let the pace of the coast take over.

Day 07

Garden Route leisure

The Knysna Heads & Featherbed

A relaxed day on the water — a cruise through the famous Knysna Heads to the Featherbed Nature Reserve, forest walks and lagoon lunches. Beaches, ice-cream and the unhurried rhythm of the coast.

OvernightWaves of GraceHerolds Bay · exclusive use
Day 08

Knysna & Sedgefield

A fynbos cottage by the lagoon

A short journey to Sedgefield and Simbavati Fynbos Sea — a cottage set between the fynbos and the lagoon, alive with birdsong. Canoe the still water, walk the beach, and watch the stars come out far from any city.

Plettenberg Bay

Robberg · Birds of Eden · Monkeyland

A day of family joy around Plettenberg Bay — the Robberg peninsula, the free-flight sanctuary of Birds of Eden and the primate forest of Monkeyland. Golden beaches close a perfect Garden Route.

Departure

Home from George

A short transfer to George Airport (GRJ) for your onward flight home via Cape Town or Johannesburg — the ocean, the forest and the whale coast carried with you.
