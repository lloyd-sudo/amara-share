Day 01

Arrival · Cape Town

Your exclusive-use city home

Met at Cape Town International, you are privately transferred to An African Story — an exclusive-use home in the heart of the city, yours alone. Settle in, and let a private chef prepare the first dinner to your family's taste.

OvernightAn African StoryCape Town · exclusive use
Day 02

Table Mountain & Bo-Kaap

The summit · Waterfront · Cape-Malay quarter

The cableway to the summit of Table Mountain, then the V&A Waterfront by the harbour. The afternoon is given to the Bo-Kaap — the historic Cape-Malay quarter, its mosques, its colour and its wealth of authentic halal kitchens.

OvernightAn African StoryCape Town · exclusive use

Day 03

Cape Point & the Peninsula

Penguins · the Cape of Good Hope

The great coastal drive — Chapman's Peak, the penguins of Boulders Beach and the wild Cape of Good Hope where two oceans meet. A harbour lunch, and an easy return to the city.

OvernightAn African StoryCape Town · exclusive use
Day 04

Constantia & Kirstenbosch

Gardens · a private valley villa

Into the green Constantia Valley — the Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens beneath Table Mountain, then your private villa at The Cellars-Hohenort, set in nine acres of gardens. An afternoon of quiet, the spa, and the oldest wine valley in the Cape.

OvernightThe Cellars-HohenortConstantia Valley

Winelands · Garden Route
Day 05

The Cape Winelands

Vergenoegd-Low · heritage & gardens

A day among the vineyards. The restored heritage estate of Vergenoegd-Low welcomes you with its gardens and daily runner-duck parade, estate lunch and grape-juice tastings — a gentle pleasure for every generation.

OvernightThe Cellars-HohenortConstantia Valley

East to the Garden Route

Herolds Bay · a clifftop villa

Travel east along the coast (with a whale-coast pause at Hermanus in season) to Waves of Grace — your exclusive-use villa on the cliffs above Herolds Bay, the Indian Ocean below.

Sedgefield & the lagoon

A fynbos cottage by the water

A short journey to Simbavati Fynbos Sea at Sedgefield — a cottage between the fynbos and the lagoon. Canoe the still water, walk the beach, and watch the stars far from any city light.

Garden Route leisure

Plettenberg · Robberg · Tsitsikamma

A final Garden Route day — the Robberg peninsula, the ancient Tsitsikamma forest and the sanctuaries of Birds of Eden and Monkeyland. Golden beaches and easy family pleasures.

To the bush

Fly George · Johannesburg · the Waterberg

A morning flight from George connects through Johannesburg, and a private transfer carries you into the Waterberg — a vast, malaria-free wilderness. Arrive at Kingfisher Villa, your exclusive-use safari home, in time for sundowners over the bush.

Waterberg · On Safari
Day 11

Waterberg Safari

Malaria-free Big Five · your private villa

The bush at dawn. In open vehicles with a private ranger, you search for the Big Five across a malaria-free reserve — safe for children — with elephant, rhino, buffalo, lion and, with fortune, leopard. Between drives, the villa is yours: pool, deck and a private chef preparing halal menus to your taste.

OvernightKingfisher VillaWaterberg · exclusive use
Day 12

Waterberg — on safari

Game drives & guided bush walks

Morning and evening game drives, a guided bush walk with your ranger, and long, slow afternoons at the villa. The children track, learn and marvel; the elders rest in complete privacy.

OvernightKingfisher VillaWaterberg · exclusive use

Waterberg — a final day

The children's highlight

One last full day on safari — a final dawn drive, perhaps a rhino and calf, and a farewell dinner beneath more stars than the city ever shows. The highlight of the whole journey for the young ones.
