Your journey, day by day
01
Arrival · Cape Town
Direct from the Gulf · private transfer to the V&A Waterfront. Cape Grace.
02
Table Mountain & the City
Cableway to the summit · V&A Waterfront · the Cape-Malay Bo-Kaap.
03
Cape Point & the Peninsula
Boulders penguins · Cape of Good Hope · the Chapman's Peak drive.
04
Winelands & leisure
Constantia & Stellenbosch estates · or a day at leisure in the city.
05
To the Whale Coast
Private transfer along the coast to Grootbos (± 2.5 hrs). Grootbos Private Nature Reserve.
06
Grootbos · marine & fynbos
Walker Bay whale watching (in season) · marine Big Five by sea.
07
Grootbos · nature & wellness
Flower safaris, horse riding & the spa amid the fynbos.
08
To the bush
Transfer to Cape Town · fly to Skukuza (SZK) · road to the lodge (± 20 min); first evening drive. Sabi Sabi Earth Lodge.
09
Sabi Sand safari
Big Five country · game drives at dawn & dusk with your private ranger.
10
Sabi Sand · a final day wild
Bush breakfasts, sundowners & the theatre of the wild.
11
Departure
Morning drive · transfer to Skukuza (SZK) · onward home.
